01 · Obsidian corpus vs rr-insights: the structural gaps

The vault (47 source documents: PRDs, KT decks, Granola exports, NPS workbooks, whiteboard photos, site-visit notes) is the system of record. rr-insights is the system of persuasion. Six gaps separate them, ranked by damage.

DimensionObsidian corpusrr-insights todayThe gap and its cost
1 · Source traceabilityEvery claim lives inside its full document; context is one scroll awayInsights carry a source name string; the document itself is unreachable from the UICRITICAL A leadership challenge ("where does 80–90% come from?") ends at a filename. Fix: sourceDoc field linking each insight to its vault note / repo doc.
2 · Freshness & driftOne note per fact; edits propagate through links automaticallyFacts hand-copied into parallel constants; sync is manualCRITICAL Proven cost: the Cohere Aug/Sep conflict and stale "upcoming" April milestones (caught v15.1). Fix: vault frontmatter → src/data export pipeline; dates exist once.
3 · Evidence densityFull verbatim transcripts and quotes51 of 267 insights carry a quote (19%); the rest are unquoted synthesisHIGH Synthesis without quotable evidence is opinion to a skeptical VP. Target: 40% quote coverage, prioritized on critical insights (112 of them, quote-first).
4 · Media evidenceWhiteboard photos, screenshots, scanned PDFs in placeZero images anywhere in the UIHIGH The Source Library describes whiteboards it cannot show. Fix: image assets for the 10 whiteboard artifacts + screenshots on competitive claims.
5 · Arbitrary queryDataview: any question over frontmatter, on demandFixed views; questions the views did not anticipate are unanswerableMEDIUM Covered by P8 global search + P7 scoring; note the vault answers "all critical Skills insights since May" today and rr-insights cannot.
6 · Bidirectional linksBacklinks free on every noteRelations computed only through shared tagsMEDIUM "This insight contradicts / supports that one" is unexpressible. Fix: optional relatedIds + relation (supports / contradicts / supersedes) — contradiction tracking is a research-practice marker almost no tool ships.
Division of labor, settled Obsidian is the working memory: raw transcripts, PII, in-progress thinking, one note per fact. rr-insights is the argument: computed, visual, stakeholder-safe, quote-backed. The pipeline between them (P9) is what makes both honest; without it, every rr-insights number is a hand copy waiting to drift.

02 · Per product: the leadership lens, the designer lens, and why the research exists

Leadership = Aarti (decision + outcome, 3 sentences), Kunal (business impact, risk, timeline), Arun (status, rationale, risk flags). Designer = what Romit must resolve to ship. "Why this research" is the sentence that justifies the repo when someone asks why a designer runs one. Evidence state is measured, and each product ends in its content fix.

Leadership cares about

FaaS 2.0

Evidence: 84 insight links · best quantified problem (95k tickets, NPS 2/5) · NO NAMED DCE OR SCCE VOICE

Leadership cares about

  • 95,000 tickets/yr as a P&L line: support cost per program, and the governance model's projected reduction
  • Compliance exposure: global settings that can break program configurations touch HIPAA/FERPA/ADA — one incident outweighs a year of tickets
  • NPS 2/5 as churn leading-indicator

Designer cares about

  • 3-level governance (CRITICAL / manager approval / warning-only) as the core interaction model
  • Reviewer UX for SCCEs: side-by-side comparison, mobile-first, zero relearning
  • Self-service form building at Google-Forms friction with SurveyMonkey capability

Why this research exists

  • 17,000 configured forms mean any redesign gamble is multiplied 17,000 times; research is the only cheap way to be wrong
  • The 95k tickets are users voting; research converts the vote into the governance spec
CONTENT FIX C2 · Two named-voice sessions (one DCE, one SCCE) before any FaaS design sprint — the largest quantified problem in the portfolio currently argues entirely from ticket counts, with no human on record.

Post Course Evaluation / Surveys
