rr-insights vs the research-repo field

Benchmark v1.0Date Jul 28, 2026 Method Competitor feature analysis + Mobbin pattern study (Dovetail, TheyDo) + rr-insights v15.1 audit Type floor in this doc 16.5px body / 13px captions (the new standard)

This benchmark answers three questions before any further build: what do enterprise research repositories actually ship, which of those capabilities matter for a one-designer five-product operation, and what do the best-in-class tools look like on screen so rr-insights stops looking like generated dashboards and starts looking like a research product. Every capability below is graded against rr-insights v15.1, and the redesign directives at the end are traced to observed patterns, not invented.

01 · The competitive landscape

The field splits into four archetypes. rr-insights is a fifth thing: a single-researcher synthesis and publication engine. That changes which features matter.

Positioning conclusion rr-insights should not chase Dovetail's team features (multi-user permissions, participant ops, live transcription). Its winning shape is: Condens's clarity + Dovetail's insight-page anatomy + TheyDo's opportunity math + Marvin's ask-the-corpus, on top of what no competitor has: a typed cross-product signal engine and audience briefing generation.

02 · Enterprise feature spec, graded against v15.1

Seven capability groups. Grade: HAVE PARTIAL MISSING NOT FOR US — "not for us" is a decision, not a gap.

Capabilityrr-insightsAssessment and requirement
A · INGESTION
Session/transcript ingestionPARTIALGranola → manual insight extraction per session. Requirement: a repeatable ingest script (already the memory workflow) formalized as scripts/ingest.mjs with per-session provenance.
Automatic transcriptionNOT FOR USGranola owns this upstream.
Survey / NPS / tracker importPARTIALNPS 2025 imported once by hand. Requirement: CSV→insight pipeline so Fall trackers and future NPS waves land without hand-copying (the source of the v15.0 misinterpretation class).
Media evidence (screens, photos, clips)MISSINGWhiteboard photos exist in project files but are not in the UI. Dovetail/Condens treat media as first-class evidence. Requirement: image evidence on insight cards and Source Library.
B · TAXONOMY & STRUCTURE
Multi-layer taxonomyHAVETags + severity + product + persona + confidence is already a stricter schema than most Dovetail workspaces maintain.
Controlled vocabulary / tag governancePARTIALTag set is implicit in data. Requirement: taxonomy.ts as the single legal tag list; ingest validates against it.
Global searchMISSINGThe number-one repository feature in every comparison, absent here. Requirement: instant client-side search over insights, sessions, arguments, milestones (Cmd+K).
C · SYNTHESIS
Insight objects with evidence chainHAVEEvidence-class labels (quote/synthesis/hypothesis) exceed the field; Dovetail does not label epistemic status.
Theme/affinity clusteringHAVESignals engine computes it; competitors mostly cluster manually or with opaque AI.
Opportunity scoring (impact × evidence)MISSINGTheyDo's pattern. Requirement: score = severity weight × evidence count × persona priority, computed, shown on signals and product pages, driving the design-next queue instead of recency.
Ask-the-corpus (natural language)MISSINGMarvin's pattern. Deliberately retired the fake Ask Claude page; the honest version is a real retrieval over insights.ts. Backlog, not fake.
D · DISTRIBUTION
No-login stakeholder accessHAVEPublic GitHub Pages URL beats every competitor's login wall for this use case.
Shareable deep linksPARTIALDrill-down state is URL-encoded on Signals only. Requirement: every page's selection state in the URL.
Audience briefings / digestsHAVEArun/Kunal/Aarti letters are ahead of the field (Condens's Insights Magazine is the nearest analog).
Embed/export (Slack, email, deck)PARTIALCopy-briefing exists. Requirement: per-insight copy-as-markdown and per-figure PNG export for decks.
E · MEASUREMENT
Research coverage / gap accountingHAVEPersona coverage bars + explicit research-debt framing; rare in the field.
Insight → outcome traceabilityMISSINGThe enterprise ROI question. Requirement: outcome field on insights linking to shipped prototypes/decisions (Magic Patterns artifact IDs), rendered as an impact trail.

03 · What the best screens actually do (Mobbin study)

Observed from Dovetail and TheyDo production screens on Mobbin. These are the directives that replace the current chart language — each one names what rr-insights does today and what it becomes.

1 · Themes are ranked bars with counts, inline with their label

Dovetail channels dashboard

Dovetail's "What customers are saying" ranks themes as full-width horizontal bars, count at the end, label at readable size on the bar's own line. No axes, no legend, no chart chrome. Replaces: the Highcharts severity stack on Signals. Theme frequency becomes a ranked bar list inside the signal index itself.

2 · Volume over time is one soft area line, not a dot field

Dovetail feedback volume

Time is shown as a single smoothed area/line per theme with a hover point, weeks on the axis. Replaces: the evidence-accumulation dot timelines (Signals Fig 3, ProductPage Fig 1), which read as generated scatter. Becomes a monthly evidence-volume area with severity split on hover.

3 · The insight page is a document with a cover, not a card in a grid

Dovetail insight detail

Title at ~28px, one-line summary, contributor + date row, then evidence as quoted highlight blocks each carrying its tags and source, then related insights. Applies to: the L2 insight card grows into this anatomy inside the evidence panel; arguments on Connect the Dots adopt the same document rhythm.

4 · Evidence is highlighted text, in context

Dovetail transcript rail

Quotes appear as color-highlighted spans inside surrounding text with the tag chip attached to the highlight. Applies to: pull quotes render as highlight spans (tinted background on the quoted words), not detached blockquotes.

5 · A digest paragraph sits above the data

Dovetail summarize panel

Dashboards open with a two-sentence written summary of what changed, then the numbers. Applies to: every page's masthead gains a computed digest line (top mover, newest critical, biggest gap) so context/goals live on the page itself, never in a separate context page.

6 · Priority is a scored matrix, not a sorted list

TheyDo opportunity scoring

Opportunities carry a visible score composed from weighted inputs, sortable, with the formula inspectable. Applies to: the design-next queue and signal ranking adopt score = severity × evidence × persona priority, with the formula shown on hover. Logical, not static.

Type scale directive (effective immediately)

Dovetail's density comes with 15–16px body text and 13px metadata. rr-insights had drifted to 9.5–12px metadata, which is illegible. New floor, shipped in v15.2: body 14px minimum anywhere, standard body 15–16px, metadata/mono 12.5px minimum, captions 13px, chart axis labels 12px, page titles unchanged. Nothing under 12px anywhere in the interface.

Chart language directive

  • Retire: multi-series stacked Highcharts bars with legends, dot-field timelines, and any chart whose first read requires a legend. These are the "generated dashboard" tells.
  • Adopt: ranked labeled bars (count-at-end), single-line volume areas, cell grids kept only where a matrix is the true shape (persona × product, parity), inline highlight spans, scored lists. All chart text at the new scale.
  • Keep: decision captions on every figure; derived-state and no-invented-numbers rules from v15.1.

The standing rule this document creates Every page owns its own context: a computed digest line states what the page is for and what changed, goals live as the decision captions on figures, and objectives are the scored queues. There is no separate "context page" to maintain, and no static paragraph that a data change can silently invalidate.
