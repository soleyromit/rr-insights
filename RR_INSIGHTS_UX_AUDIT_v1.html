01 · Diagnosis: why it reads as "so much text"

These are structural causes, not cosmetic ones. Fixing typography will not fix this.

Root cause 1 — Append-only growth with no synthesis layer

Every Granola sync since v8.0 appended new insight text into pages. Nothing was ever compressed, retired, or promoted. 292 insights are rendered as 292 cards. A research repo's job is the opposite: many sources in, few defended findings out. The Connect the Dots view (5 arguments) is the only page that does this, and it is buried mid-sidebar as one item among 23.

Root cause 2 — The data model is rich, the rendering is flat

Insights carry tags, severity, products, personas, and sources. Almost no page exploits this structure. Instead of a persona × product heat map computed from the data, there are hand-written paragraphs describing the same relationships. The evidence exists as structured data and is presented as prose. This is why nothing connects: connections are computable but never computed.

Root cause 3 — Navigation encodes chronology, not meaning

The sidebar is a changelog. Items like Exam Admin Audit · New, Arun Performance · New, Nav IA · Apr 1, Intelligence Analytics · D3+Plot were each added the week they were built. Badge soup ("New", "★", "D3+Plot", "5 arguments", "-47 NPS") signals recency of construction, not importance of content. A first-time stakeholder cannot tell which 3 of the 23 items matter.

Root cause 4 — Tab explosion inside product pages

Exam Management: 1,803 lines, 16 tabs (insights, blueprint, userflows, features, analytics, accessibility, competitive, decisions, gaps, stories, pa-dashboard, scalable-viz, story-view, arun-roadmap, question-bank, analogy). Tabs at this count are a filing cabinet, not a narrative. Several tabs duplicate global pages (competitive, analytics, roadmap), so the same finding lives in 3 places with no canonical home.

Root cause 5 — No entry point answers "what should I do?"

The skill's north star is a loop ending in action: measure → improve → build → change. No page currently ends in "therefore, design this next." The Overview shows platform stats about the repo itself (sessions synced, insights count) rather than the state of the products. The repo reports on its own bookkeeping instead of on Exxat's users.

The one-line diagnosis rr-insights optimized for capture completeness when its value is synthesis density. Every fix below converts stored text into computed, visual, decision-pointing structure.

03 · Proposed information architecture

Four layers, ordered by the question each answers. Story before evidence, evidence before raw material, outputs last. 23 items become 11.

04 · User flows: three audiences, three journeys

The repo has exactly three real audiences. Each gets a designed path with a designed exit. Today all three land on the same bookkeeping Overview and wander.

05 · Interaction model: one drill-down, everywhere

The single pattern that makes 292 insights navigable instead of overwhelming. Everything is a progressive disclosure of the same data model.

06 · Page anatomy: the storytelling template

Every product page follows a four-act structure. Exam Management's 16 tabs collapse into this. Acts, not tabs: vertical scroll with a sticky act-tracker, because a narrative has an order and tabs deny order.

Act 1 · The Stakes

Hero · 1 screen · 8-second scan

One hero question ("Can a student with accommodations complete a lockdown exam without leaving the app?"), one hero stat, the deadline countdown, and the persona most affected. No cards. No lists.

Exam: UNF pilot blocked until a11y V1 · FaaS: 95k tickets · Skills: 80–90% spreadsheet exodus · PCE: 7 surveys outside Exxat

Act 2 · The Evidence

Signals · drill-down entry

Top 3–5 signals for this product as L0 cards (Section 05), a computed persona-friction strip, and the product's slice of the NPS drivers. All insight text lives behind the drill-down, never on the surface.

Act 3 · The Design Response

Decisions · diagrams first

Key decisions with tradeoffs (skill template 6.1), the product's one canonical flow diagram (governance flow for FaaS, routing for PCE, accommodation stack for Exam), and Magic Patterns prototype embeds/links with status.

Act 4 · The Scoreboard

Progress · finish line

Success metrics from skill Section 6 rendered as current → target with a trend, competitive parity slice, and the open-questions list. Exam Admin Audit content lands here as a collapsible evidence panel.

Tab disposal map for Exam Management Act 1: stories, analogy (compressed to one narrative) · Act 2: insights, gaps, pa-dashboard · Act 3: blueprint, userflows, features, accessibility, question-bank, scalable-viz, story-view · Act 4: analytics, decisions, arun-roadmap · Global pages: competitive → Competitive Parity. Sixteen tabs, zero content lost, one story gained.

Chart discipline (from skill Section 7, now enforced)

  • Heat map grid only for persona × product friction. Radar only for competitor parity and persona coverage. Timeline only for the roadmap. Horizontal bars only for severity counts. Donut only for per-product tag composition, capped at one per product page.
  • Every chart ships with a one-line caption stating the decision it supports. A chart that cannot state its decision gets cut in review. This document models the rule: each figure above carries its justification.
  • Meta-charts about the repo itself (insight counts, session counts) are exiled to Changelog. Stakeholders never see the bookkeeping.

Non-AI aesthetic enforcement

  • Type: DM Serif Display for page titles and hero stats, IBM Plex Mono for data, labels, captions, Source Sans 3 for body. Kill remaining system-ui/Inter fallbacks.
  • Color: product accents stay (Exam violet, FaaS teal, PCE amber, Skills blue, LC purple); severity gets the semantic ramp; all data marks are neutral gray until hovered. No gradients on data. The one existing gradient (sidebar logo) is the single permitted decorative element.
  • Badges: sidebar badges limited to two types: deadline countdown (mono, e.g. 34d) and severity dot. "New", "★", "D3+Plot" and other construction-recency badges removed.
  • Density: hard rule of one idea per screen-height in Act 1; evidence density permitted only inside drill-downs.
  • Motion: drill-down panel slides at 180ms ease-out; heat grid cells scale 1.02 on hover; nothing else animates. Clinical-grade restraint per skill Section 10.

Content strategy rules (Lens 9)

  • Every insight card labels its evidence class: DIRECT QUOTE, SYNTHESIS, or HYPOTHESIS, with confidence. Research credibility comes from this honesty, and it is currently invisible.
  • Accreditation vocabulary (CAPTE, SCCE, CPI, FWPE, ARC-PA) used verbatim in UI copy; the glossary lives as hover definitions, not a page.
  • Empty and thin states written as research debt ("2 sessions cover Learning Contracts; the SCCE voice is absent; next agenda: …"), never padded to appear complete.
